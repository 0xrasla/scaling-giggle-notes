Roasted Daily

From green beans to your cup in 48 hours

We started Kettle & Bean with a single drum roaster and a stubborn belief: coffee shouldn't sit in a warehouse for months before it reaches you. Every batch is roasted in-house each morning and rested just long enough to bloom.

We buy direct from small farms we visit, pay well above commodity rates, and cup every lot before it earns a spot on the menu. It's slower, and that's the point.
