Our story

Roasted three blocks from where you drink it.

We opened Hearth & Bean in 2016 with one roaster, a secondhand espresso machine, and a stubborn belief that a coffee shop should feel like a living room — one that happens to serve excellent coffee.

Every bean is sourced directly from farms we can name, roasted in small batches on our own roaster, and served within days of roasting. Nothing sits on a shelf waiting to go stale. Nothing is rushed.
