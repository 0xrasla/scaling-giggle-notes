How we roast

Four stages, one slow morning.

A roast is a quiet, four-stage arc. We watch every one — the color change is our clock.

01 — GREEN

In the drum

Grass-fresh beans arrive and roll into the drum at room temperature. Nothing happening yet — just potential.

INIT 24°C

02 — MAILLARD

Heat builds

Sugars and amino acids start reacting. The bean dries, then turns tan as the chemistry of flavor wakes up.

DRY 165°C

03 — FIRST CRACK

The pop

The beans crackle audibly and open. The roast takes shape from here — this is where the drummer's ear matters.

CRACK 196°C

04 — DEVELOPMENT

Rest & pack

We drop, cool, rest for 72 hours, and pack within 24 more. Freshness is the whole point.

DROP 204°C
