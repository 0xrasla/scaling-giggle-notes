The curve, by the numbers

Process board
STEP 01

Charge

~205°C into the drum

Twelve kilos of green beans hit the drum at full heat. The first audible settle is the drum finding its rhythm.

STEP 02

Dry / Maillard

140–180°C

Moisture steams off and sugars begin to brown. This is where the bean starts to give up its green, grassy edge.

STEP 03

First crack

~196°C

The bean pops open like fresh popcorn — the first real sign it has become coffee. We listen for the rate, not just the sound.

STEP 04

Drop

~208°C

We pull the batch at the sweet spot, before the oils push to the surface and the cup goes dull. Green is now amber.
