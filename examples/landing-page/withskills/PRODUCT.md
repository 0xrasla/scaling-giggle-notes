# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Local coffee lovers in the neighborhood — people who want a great coffee nearby, care about where their beans come from, and can be convinced to drop by the bar. They are looking for a place, not a lecture: the page should make them feel the craft is real and the welcome is warm.

## Product Purpose

First Crack is a single-origin coffee roastery and bar (est. 2016). The roastery roasts small 12 kg batches to first crack — pulled at the moment the bean opens up, never past it. The bar pours those roasts and sells the beans to go. The landing page's job is to drive visits to the bar: a first-time visitor should know what this place is, why it matters, and where/when to come, within seconds.

## Positioning

We roast slowly, precisely, and to the exact moment the bean opens up. "Green in, amber out — pulled at first crack, never past it." Single-origin only, no blends. This precision/ethos is the memorable claim; the roast curve and drum language are the visual signature.

## Operating Context

- A working roastery and cafe. People experience the drum, the roast curve, the smell, and the bar.
- The roast process has four named stages: Charge (~205°C), Dry/Maillard (140–180°C), First Crack (~196°C), Drop (~208°C).
- Bar serves espresso drinks, filter/V60, cold brew, batch filter, and beans to go (250 g bags, whole or ground; monthly subscription).
- Hours: Mon–Fri 07:00–18:00, Sat 08:00–19:00, Sun 08:00–16:00. Address: 42 Roastery Lane, Old Town District.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static single landing page (`index.html`), no framework, no build step.
- Web platform, dark color-scheme baseline, prefers-reduced-motion already respected.
- Content (origins, menu, prices, hours, roast data) is invented placeholder material for this demo and is authorable demo content — not factual claims that must be preserved verbatim.
- All content must remain non-deceptive: invented prices/menu are fine as illustrative demo material, but nothing may claim real customers, real locations, or real sourcing beyond what the page already states.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: FIRST CRACK — Coffee Roasters & Bar. Existing wordmark uses FIRST in solid + CRACK in outlined/stroke type.
- Existing voice: terse, craft-proud, slightly wry ("Come find your crack"). Preserve the name and this voice register.
- No external brand assets, logos, or photography provided.

## Evidence on Hand

- The existing `index.html` surface (the incumbent visual world being replaced).
- No real photography, no real customer proof, no real price sheet. Do not fabricate testimonials, press, or location claims. Authorable demo content (menu, prices, origins, hours, roast curve data) is the exception and may be authored at production fidelity and labeled as such where a visitor could mistake it for real.

## Product Principles

- Precision is the brand: the first-crack moment is the point of pride, never understated.
- Local and warm: a neighborhood bar first, a roastery second.
- No blends, no compromise: single-origin truth runs through every section.
- Show the craft working (the drum, the curve, the stages), don't just claim it.
- Respect the visitor's time: the visit path (where, when, go) is always one glance away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Respect prefers-reduced-motion (already present in incumbent).
- Dark/light contrast must pass for body text and small mono labels.
- Keyboard-focusable navigation and skip link preserved.
