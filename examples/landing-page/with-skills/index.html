The 1869

A fungus the size of a thumbprint ended an empire.

The island that fed the world's coffee habit lost it all in a single decade.

In 1869, coffee rust — Hemileia vastatrix — reached Ceylon's wet hills. The island was then the world's largest coffee producer; within a decade the plantations were gone. The planters ripped out the coffee and planted tea. The world moved on.

The kades didn't.

1869The rust lands. Vastatrix takes hold in the hill country within one season.
1877The collapse. Output craters; estates replant with tea at scale.
1900sThe forgetting. Ceylon becomes the world's tea garden. Coffee becomes a memory.
TodayThe revival. A few estates plant coffee again, and we roast it in Colombo.

Tea made the island famous. Coffee made it first.
